There are 8 aspects a creature can have, 5 elemental aspects, and 3 meta aspects. You can think of aspects as the core building blocks of a creatures being and essence.

The Elemental aspects are Fire, Earth, Lightning, Water and Air.
The Meta aspects are Void, Aether and Chaos.

The elemental aspects form a closed loop
*Fire > Earth > Lightning > Water > Air > Fire*
While meta aspects exist outside of that loop
Void <> Aether
Void and Aether oppose each other and ignore the defensive bonus you would receive from 'trumping' an aspect, moves are always effective
Chaos exists outside of this as a solitary aspect with no weakness or strength
