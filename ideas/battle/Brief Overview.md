You have 3 active creature slots
Each slot can hold up to two of your creatures.
Only of of those two creatures can be active at any one time, but can be tagged in and out
Meaning you can have 3 creatures out at once, each one having a tag partner

Your player and each creature can have up to 4 moves active at any one time, accessed by the 4 primary face buttons on a controller (Keyboard tbc, LMB, RMB, Q, E?).
By default these 4 buttons would activate the players moves
To access creature moves you would use modifier buttons
L1 - Creature Slot 1
R1 - Creature Slot 2
L1+R1 - Creature Slot 3
(Keyboard tbc Ctrl, Shift, Alt?)

Consider something similar to FFXIIs gambit system to automatically execute abilities

To control your active slots you will use the D-pad Up/Down or mouse wheel to select the slot
Right D-pad or R - Tag out the current creature and swap with partner if possible
Left D-pad or F - Tag out the current creature

Consider what might happen when a creature dies, should the next creature automatically replace it, no? Configurable?
