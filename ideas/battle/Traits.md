Traits are flags each creature has that define it.  You can think of these as labels that define a creatures biology and behaviour.

So in that sense traits can be anything, Flying, Dragon, Beast, Spirit etc. And that's where the complexity lies, as this will be the main driving force of most synergies.

What type of traits could exist?
Biological: Beast, Insect, Dragon, Plant etc.
Behavioural: Predator, Pack, Solitary, Territorial
Environmental: Aquatic, Nocturnal, Forest, Desert
Metaphysical: Spirit, Celestial, Mechanical
Role: Guardian, Support, Attacker, etc.

While traits can be unlimited it makes sense that creatures in early zones will have a  low number of traits, for both balancing and to not overwhelm the player. While such a system might be less exciting to veterans.
