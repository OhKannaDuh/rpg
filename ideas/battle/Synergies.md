Synergies can exist both between the active team and partners.
Read more about teams here: [[Brief Overview]]

Synergies are activated through a few things depending on your team make up, the deciding factor will be
- Creature Aspect (Fire/Water etc.)
- Creature Species
- Creature Traits (Beast/Flying)
- Lore specific (Hunter/Prey combo, related in some kind of story, This grouping is pretty extensive and is almost a catch all)

As a basic design principal synergies should never be totally negative, as that not only discourages certain team make ups, but also negatively impacts more casual players not totally engaging in the synergy system. That being said, synergies can have downsides to counteract their positive sides.


Team synergies exist between your 2 or 3 active creatures
Partner synergies exist between the two creatures that share the same creature slot

In both cases a creature must be alive to provide for the synergy. Though there may be some exceptions to this rule.
- A lone-wolf synergy, active when the creature has no partner in its tag slot
- A Spirit-bonded synergy, benefit from the spirit of a dead ally (Tag partner)
Those are just some ideas.
