
As with any game the main focus will be on the battle system [[Brief Overview]] but we should offer a variety of other content for players to engage with.

One of my main design pillars is to make the game playable and fun for players of all tiers. Meaning difficulty is opt in. One primary example of this is making survival features opt in, such as hunger, thirst, environmental effects. Often these can be seen as tedious and gating by some, therefore those specific example will be opt out.

## Sanctuary

The sanctuary system will be a player built world, allowing for them to build their own home, a place for their creatures to hang out, a place for them to relax. Also a place to engage with other systems such as creature breeding, farming and other lifestyle skills.

## Creature Relationships

Creatures in a players party will have a relationship with that player, on a basic level this could just be a friendship level. TBC what this impacts.

Creatures also have relationships with other creature's the player owns.
